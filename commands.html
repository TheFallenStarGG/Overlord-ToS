Reference

Commands

Everything below uses the !! prefix. In Discord, !!help is always more up to date than this page if something just shipped.

Economy

!!balance Your coins
!!daily Daily reward
!!work Work for coins
!!career Career / job progress
!!rob Rob someone (if enabled)
!!give Give coins
!!trade Trade with another member
!!leaderboard Top balances
!!rank / !!profile Your rank card
!!prestige Prestige reset path
!!lottery Lottery
!!quests Quests

Shop, gathering, crafting

!!shop / !!buy Buy items
!!sell Sell fish, ore, crafts
!!inventory / !!use Bag and use items
!!fish / !!mine Gather resources
!!craft Craft food, tools, trinkets
!!forge Weapons and armor
!!pet Pets

Stocks & world info

!!stocks Market board / news
!!invest / !!cashout Buy and sell shares
!!portfolio Your holdings
!!ipo Player companies
!!forecast Weather
!!board Today in the Realm snapshot
!!gazette Daily paper
!!changelog Bot updates

Combat & competition

!!duel Fight another player
!!boss Boss fights
!!loadout Gear and class
!!bounty Active mark (if available)
!!rebellion / !!usurper Throne / rebellion
!!ranked / !!tournament Ranked play
!!stats Combat stats

Games & misc

!!wordle and other game commands
!!poll !!roll !!flip !!choose Small utilities
!!tutorial In-bot guide
!!invite Bot invite link
!!support Support server
!!report / !!feedback Send feedback to the owner
!!deletedata Request data deletion

Admin (Manage Server)

!!settings Gambling, rob, channels, command toggles, board
!!events-channel Announcement feed (or via settings)
!!edittitles Custom shop titles / roles
!!reactionroles Reaction role panels

Full channel setup notes are on the Setup page.
